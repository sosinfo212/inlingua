# Client verification required before publishing

The audit recommends the claims below, but they are intentionally **not asserted as facts in this build**. Confirm wording, evidence, scope, and current status before adding them to public pages or structured data.

- Founding date, including “since 1986,” and any years-in-business calculation
- “100+ languages” or any exact language/interpreter count
- ATA-certified translators or other individual/provider certifications
- MBE, SBPP, COMMBUYS, SAM, or other supplier registrations and identifiers
- HIPAA compliance, “HIPAA-ready workflows,” EMR experience, and confidentiality/security guarantees
- 24/7, emergency, same-day, or guaranteed turnaround availability
- “Certified translation,” USCIS acceptance, court acceptance, notarization, or legal-compliance claims
- Client/partner names and logos, including utilities, hospitals, agencies, schools, and companies
- Testimonials, Google review ratings, case studies, past performance, awards, and measurable outcomes
- inlingua International affiliation and the permitted way to describe or display it
- Staff credentials, instructor qualifications, course hours, certificates, diplomas, schedules, tuition, and career outcomes
- ASL service or training availability
- Service geography and any claim of nationwide coverage
- Insurance certificates, W-9 availability, procurement contacts, capability statement, NAICS codes, and vendor onboarding details
- AI-enabled or AI-enhanced workflows and the human-review/security controls surrounding them
- Official address, phone, email, hours, accessibility details, and social profiles
